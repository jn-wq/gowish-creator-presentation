The Partnership Model

The Commercial Model

How everyone gets paid.

01
20% commission on tracked sales
Creator earns 20% on every sale attributed through GoWish. No flat fees, no minimum guarantees. Only pays when GoWish delivers paying customers.
02
GoWish funds & runs paid social
Meta and TikTok ad spend covered entirely by GoWish. We create, run, and optimise the campaigns. Zero ad budget required from the brand.
03
Creator licenses likeness for ads
Creator grants GoWish a scoped, time-bound licence to use their image in campaign ads. As the founder of the brand, this is authentic storytelling — not a paid endorsement.
